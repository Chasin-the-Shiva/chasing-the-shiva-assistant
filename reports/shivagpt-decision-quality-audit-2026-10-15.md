## Decision quality safeguards
- Yahoo league scoring and IDs are authoritative; generic PPR is not a scoring substitute.
- Keepers and only starter at a scarce position cannot be dropped without deliberate strategic review.
- Require verified current FA/W status, exact drop candidate and current FAAB.
- Priority to sustainable offensive opportunities and prospective trade value, especially running backs; return-only scoring is temporary FLEX support.
- If workload, injury, snaps, routes, target share, or expected role is missing, label RESEARCH REQUIRED, not recommended ADD.
- FAAB target and maximum remain null until current role, market price, player value and relative roster improvement are established.
- Doing nothing is an acceptable recommended outcome.
- Wednesday candidates need post-processing Yahoo confirmation of status FA and $0 FAAB cost.
